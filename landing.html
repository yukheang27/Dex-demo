Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MeetNotes AI

Is my meeting data secure and private? ▼
Yes, we take security very seriously. All meetings are encrypted in transit and at rest with enterprise-grade security. We comply with GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2 Type II standards. Your data is never used to train our AI models.
What meeting platforms does MeetNotes support? ▼
We work with all major platforms including Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Slack Huddles, and many more. You can also upload audio files for transcription.
How accurate is the transcription? ▼
Our AI achieves 99.9% accuracy in English and 95%+ accuracy across other languages. It handles multiple speakers, accents, and technical terminology with ease.
Can I customize the summary format? ▼
Yes! You can customize summary templates, tone, length, and focus areas. You can also use our API to build custom integrations.
Do you offer a free trial? ▼
Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial of our Professional plan with full access to all features. No credit card required to get started.
